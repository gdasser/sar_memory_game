Rules & reference

Goal: collect the most satellites.

  1. Study the satellite card for one minute.
  2. When time is up, the card is hidden.
  3. Roll the die. The number determines the question.
  4. Answer correctly to collect the satellite.
  5. If incorrect, the satellite returns to the deck and the turn passes.

Reading the acquisition & availability line

First word — how the data is acquired / who it serves:

global: systematically acquired at global scale.

on-demand / tasked: acquired on specific user request.

national / government: primarily serves the operating nation / its agencies.

Second word — how you can get the data:

open archive: openly available for research and applications.

agreements: available under research / education agreements.

commercial: sold commercially (may have research/education terms).

dual-use: civilian and defence use.

restricted: available only to specific countries / users.

unavailable: not generally distributed.
